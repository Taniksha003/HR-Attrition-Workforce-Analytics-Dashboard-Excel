# HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics 
## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition patterns and workforce metrics using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides key HR insights to help organizations understand employee turnover, identify high-risk groups, and support retention strategies.

---

## Objective

The goal of this project is to:

- Monitor employee attrition trends
- Analyze workforce demographics
- Identify departments and job roles with high attrition
- Evaluate the impact of overtime, income level, and tenure on employee turnover
- Present insights through an interactive dashboard

## Key Insights

- Research & Development accounts for 56% of total employee attrition.
- 68% of employees leave within their first 5 years.
- Employees aged 26–35 experience the highest attrition.
- Employees working overtime contribute to 54% of total attrition.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Improve retention strategies within the Research & Development department.
2. Strengthen onboarding and employee engagement programs.
3. Review workload distribution to reduce excessive overtime.
4. Provide career development opportunities for employees aged 26–35.
5. Focus on retaining employees during their first five years of employment.
